## Visual decisions

1. **Composition:** a wide desktop now presents attack and defence as two coequal entry cards rather than a tall sequential stack.
2. **Primary journey:** the new workflow turns the overview from a descriptive landing page into an analyst starting point with three explicit next actions.
3. **Density:** framework metrics use a readable shelf on wide screens and a deliberate compact grid on mobile, preserving labels and line length.
